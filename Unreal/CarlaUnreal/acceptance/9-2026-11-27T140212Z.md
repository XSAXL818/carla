## Problems found (adversarial)

Nothing blocking. Four notes, in decreasing importance, each with a one-line reproduction:

- **F1 (non-blocking, one unexplained observation — please look):** on the panel's **first**
  load window of a session, the *close* produced **no** `?force=true` probe, while every later
  close did. Repro: fresh panel + stub that logs `/health` queries → at startup the panel POSTs
  `/sessions/preview_map` (`21:52:41.204`), the window closes, and the next `/health` is a plain
  poll (`21:52:48.171`) with no `force=true` in between; clear a later `preview_loading` window and
  `force=true` arrives within ~0.2 s. Consequence is benign — the chip stays `未知` (never a false
  `未连接`) until the next 5 s poll — but the commit message's "窗口结束时立刻强制重探" does not hold
  for that one path, and I could not derive the mechanism from the source (`SyncCarlaProbeWindow`
  is reached on both paths I can see). It needs a look, not a rework.
- **F2 (non-blocking, hygiene):** the 18 evidence images named in the task are **untracked**
  (`?? Unreal/CarlaUnreal/Source/TestPlatform/Platform/ui/panel-addr-*.png`); earlier rounds put
  their evidence into `acceptance/` and committed it (`8cc1060ac`, `00e368a54`). The product
  deliverables (the 5 source files) are tracked; the evidence is not. Reproduce: `git status --porcelain`.
- **F3 (non-blocking, latent):** the learned endpoint is never invalidated — `InvalidateCarlaState()`
  clears `bCarlaKnown`, not `bCarlaEndpointKnown`. If a backend answered without `host`/`port` after
  an earlier one did, the chip would keep the old address next to a live state. Not reachable with
  the shipped backend (`main.py:70-75` always includes both); reproduced only with a stub that drops
  the fields after having sent them.
- **F4 (nit, comment vs behaviour):** `UpdateChrome()`'s comment says "a probe that *succeeds*
  during it is still displayed as 已连接", but the window also pauses polling (`TickRefresh`), so a
  successful probe cannot normally be observed during the window; the chip is `未知` even when CARLA
  is fine (which is the intended, conservative choice). Repro: open a `preview_loading` window and
  watch `/health` — zero requests for the whole window (`stub-requests.log`, 21:56:30→21:57:10).
